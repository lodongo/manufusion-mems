## Implementation invariants
- Maintain WR review outcome close or confirm (and existing cancellation semantics). Confirmation creates WO; assessment is its first operational step. Avoid a new standalone assessment queue. Preserve simplified safety sign-off and existing spares/services closure integrity. Work order can close early where resource and safety prerequisites permit.
- Maintenance Management navigation: Dashboard, Work Requests, Work Orders, Maintenance Windows, Maintenance Groups, Setup; retain Work Centre functionality. Setup holds governance, performance targets, capacity, time classifications and activity standards. Do not duplicate existing platform metadata or approval engines.
- Three independent organization-level switches default OFF: WR approval before conversion; WO approval at selected stage; technical closure approval after safety sign-off and applicable resource reconciliation. No extra technical closure step if disabled. Approval state is distinct from operational readiness. Disabled approvals must not block operations. Keep existing mandatory safety and financial controls independent. Register workflow and role permissions in the existing engine; do not introduce a parallel approval engine.
- At transaction creation persist immutable effective governance/workflow version and approval configuration. New configuration applies only to records created after activation. Existing transactions retain their actual legacy workflow; a newly created WO follows its own creation-time policy; do not retroactively rewrite closed records, create duplicate workflows, or infer missing historical decisions. Define treatment of existing generic mandatory assessment gates by preserving original records' policy while applying new optional WO approval to newly created records. Validate stage and role mappings before activation; fail closed for misconfigured enabled approvals.
- Cleanup: development only, no live data; explicit separate enablement, session-bound, server-side 60 minute maximum, revoke at logout and Test Mode disable/version change. Revalidate under lock before deletion; expiry/role/org/version changes invalidate previews. UI must not offer deletion while unauthorized. Preview selected related records, typed confirmation and reason; retain separate immutable cleanup receipt even where ordinary linked test audit rows can be removed. Keep existing safeguards for protected posted stock/accounting/shared masters. Do not execute actual deletion as part of this release. No test-mode bypass can bypass the cleanup guard.
- Core reporting uses tenant scoped server-side authorization, not client filters alone. Drill Organization > Country > Legal Entity > Site > Department > Cost Centre > relevant Work Centre/maintenance group/line/functional area > Equipment > Assembly > transaction; employee dimension cross-cuts hierarchy and follows assignment/operating scope, not a forced equipment child. Use actual organizational relationships. Prevent duplicates when aggregating multiple dimensions and cross-group assistance. No salary-level details in general analytics.
- Period menu: Today, Yesterday, Current Week, Last Week, Current Month (financial period), Last Month, Current Year (FYTD), Last Year; custom date ranges and equivalent previous-period comparisons. Hourly/day, daily/week, weekly/financial period, monthly or configured financial periods/year. Establish explicit alignment for consolidated entities with unlike calendars and disclose different boundaries; do not silently mix incomparable dates. Local site time for event display; persist timestamps in UTC.
- WR metrics: absolute creation, conversion, cancellation, closed without conversion, outstanding and overdue; cohort conversion = converted requests created within the selected period / created requests (as of report cutoff); throughput = conversions occurring within selected period / creations occurring within period; zero denominator = N/A; throughput may exceed 100%. Explicitly distinguish as-of stock from period event flow. WO metrics: created, closed, cancelled, backlog, overdue, planned/unplanned, schedule compliance, readiness, delay, costs and labour. Open backlog = opening + created - terminal transitions +/- reopen and scope reconciliation. Use exact existing operational state precedence and expose distinct blockers (spares before services/specialists, then scheduling/execution/closure; approval at configured stage).
- Historical state: append-only event with record, previous/new state, UTC timestamp, actor, source, reason and effective policy version; idempotent same-state events; reconstruct as-of status including terminal events, and state duration over interval. Do not fabricate pre-instrumentation state or coverage. Build incremental scoped aggregates and drill-through which reconcile to source; distinguish elapsed lifecycle, actual artisan man-hours and equipment downtime. Show data coverage and missing values.
- Dashboard: colorful compact mobile-first KPI cards, time trends, live/historical status distribution, stacked history, backlog ageing, Pareto/delay chart, cost composition, target markers and previous-period comparison. Separate WR and WO reports; reliability, cost, work centre and employee views. Click metrics, cross-filter, drill to underlying records, preserve filters on return; authorization-checked export and actions. Dashboard preferences cannot expand permissions.
- Cost: approved estimates, outstanding commitments, posted actuals, remaining forecast without double count, cost categories, budget variance and currency conversion using existing LE/reference rules. Internal labour from approved rates and actual time; external services/specialists, inventory issues/returns, hire and configured overhead. Missing source/rates marked pending, never silently zero. Separate operational forecast from accounting posting; preserve ledger controls.
- Reliability: MTBF requires credible operating time and failures; MTTR requires valid repair interval; availability requires valid operating/planned time basis; report downtime, repeat failures, bad actors, failure modes, RCA and strategy effectiveness. Missing denominators return N/A, no fabricated figures.
- Artisan eligibility is defined by maintenance execution roles. Capacity is effective-dated: contractual hours/day, days/week, shift roster, calendar, approved overtime, leave/holidays/other non-available deductions, target loading %. Deduplicate overlapping exclusions and overtime. Net available = scheduled hours + approved overtime - deductible non-available hours. Target loadable = net available x loading %. Loading = planned man-hours / net available; utilization = actual execution man-hours / net available; target achievement = planned / loadable; efficiency = earned approved standard man-hours / actual matched execution man-hours. Report zero denominators N/A and aggregate by sums, not averages of individual rates. Distinguish direct maintenance from total productive time; do not penalize employees for external delays or insufficient allocations.
- Individual time bookings at WO activity level: start/pause/resume/complete and manual/supervisor entries; multiple artisans on one activity, no overlapping bookings per employee; cross-shift/timezone intervals, partial completion, reassignment and approved correction audit. Separate elapsed job time from parallel man-hours. Full-day classification (productive, nonproductive, deductible, attributable delay), non-WO bookings, optional timesheet verification and approvals. Preserve original time entries and correction history; avoid duplicate time attribution.
- Scheduling: planner sees effective net and loadable capacity, existing assignments, skills, equipment competencies and leave. Warn vs target; prevent conflicting/unavailable/over-capacity assignments, except authorized documented overrides where appropriate; never permit actual double-booked execution. Suggest alternative qualified people; support future periods, shutdowns, approved overtime, interactive resource schedule and shift coverage.
- Activity library: reusable task templates, equipment/assembly overrides, standard duration/manpower/man-hours, tools and competencies; version/effective date and approval. Snapshot approved standard on activity; historical recommendations require adequate comparable samples and human approval; never auto-change standards or judge efficiency without valid standard.

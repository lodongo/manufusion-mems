Date: 2026-10-24
Status: Draft feature PR #47, depends on P1 #44 → P2 #45 → P3 #46. No production deployment.

## Architecture
Do not alter the legal hierarchy. A legal entity owns its own Finance functional currency, fiscal calendar, approved GL chart and bank accounts. Separate operating/reporting/administrative mapping does not make it an accounting entity.

Configuration in Finance → Corporate Budgets & Allocation → Intercompany & Treasury.

### Approved contract registry
Each bilateral contract has a provider, beneficiary, transaction classes, document currency, approved maximum, accounting roles for each side, signed source agreement, dates, settlement policy, tax and pricing references. The provider and beneficiary have independent Finance/CoA approvals. A contract's approval creates no liability, asset, revenue or expenditure.

### Economic event types
- SERVICE_RECHARGE: Provider Dr IC receivable / Cr service recovery; beneficiary Dr service expense / Cr IC payable.
- COST_REIMBURSEMENT: Provider Dr IC receivable / Cr reimbursement clearing; beneficiary Dr reimbursement clearing / Cr IC payable. Requires verification of underlying purchase and prior expense postings.
- LOAN_ADVANCE: Provider Dr loan receivable / Cr bank; beneficiary Dr bank / Cr loan payable.
- LOAN_REPAYMENT: Provider Dr bank / Cr loan receivable; beneficiary Dr loan payable / Cr bank.
- CAPITAL_CONTRIBUTION: Provider Dr equity investment / Cr bank; beneficiary Dr bank / Cr share capital. Corporate-law formalities apply; no automatic issuance of share capital.
- ON_BEHALF_PAYMENT: Provider Dr IC receivable / Cr bank; beneficiary Dr reimbursement clearing / Cr IC payable. Requires verified original liability/expense and proof of third-party disbursement.

These are journal INTENT examples, not instructions executed by this phase; actual treatment depends on agreement facts, indirect tax, withholding tax, FX, local statutes and certified chart mappings.

### Matched vouchers and controls
1. Finance officer creates source document and voucher. Code validates amount/ceiling, transaction type, support files, tax-review reference and FX rates.
2. Independent provider Finance approver authorizes under amount-based CoA.
3. Beneficiary officer requests matching with independent Finance approval. Provider maker and approver cannot act as beneficiary matcher.
4. Approved mirrored journal intents have independently balanced provider/beneficiary amounts in their own functional currencies.
5. Tax evidence, capital/legal approvals, period status, account mappings and invoice documentation must be checked at actual posting.

Voucher matched_ready means both parties agreed on a potential transaction; it does not mean posted. No invoice, journal, supplier liability, tax document or accounting receivable/payable is generated by matching.

### Settlement planning
A matched voucher may have one or more Finance-approved settlement plan intents, bounded by voucher document amount. Settlement methods are bank transfer, netting, pool or manual. Treasury instructions, bank payments and ledger clearing are explicitly disabled; a plan does not prove payment or satisfy a loan.
